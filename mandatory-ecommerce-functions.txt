MANDATORY ECOMMERCE FUNCTIONS MISSING

These are the functions that are really important for making the platform usable as a proper ecommerce website.

1. Admin product management UI
- Admin should be able to add, edit, delete, and manage products from the website.
- This should include product name, price, stock, category, images, description, and status.

2. Real product image upload
- Current upload route is only a placeholder.
- The platform needs real image upload and storage for product photos.

3. Product variants
- Products should support size, color, fabric, or other options.
- Stock should ideally be managed per variant/SKU.

4. Razorpay webhook support
- Payment verification currently depends mainly on frontend flow.
- Razorpay webhooks are needed to confirm payments reliably even if the customer closes the browser or the frontend fails.

5. Shipping and tracking
- Admin should be able to add courier name, tracking number, and shipping status.
- Customers should be able to see tracking details on their order page.

6. Invoice or bill generation
- Customers should be able to view or download an invoice for their order.
- This is important for trust, records, and accounting.

7. Password reset
- Users need a forgot password / reset password function.
- Without this, customers can get locked out of their accounts.

8. Legal and policy pages
- Privacy Policy
- Terms and Conditions
- Shipping Policy
- Cancellation / Refund / Return Policy
- These are important for trust and legal clarity.

9. Return or exchange handling
- If returns/exchanges are allowed, the platform needs a request and approval flow.
- If returns/exchanges are not allowed, the policy should be clearly shown and enforced.

10. Order notifications
- Customers should receive updates for:
  - Order placed
  - Payment successful or failed
  - Order shipped
  - Order delivered
  - Order cancelled
  - Refund initiated or processed
- Notifications can be email, SMS, or WhatsApp.
