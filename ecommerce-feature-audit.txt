SUMMARY

The platform already has the core ecommerce flow:
catalog, product detail, login, cart, wishlist, checkout, COD, Razorpay payment,
orders, cancellation, refunds, stock handling, and an admin dashboard.

The biggest missing areas are:
product variants, real admin product/category management UI, real image upload,
shipping/tracking integration, coupons, notifications, invoices, reviews,
password reset, Razorpay webhooks, and return/exchange management.
